Import progress from your save file PC

Fill in your quests, recipes, urns, lore scraps, agility medals and skill levels automatically from your character save. The file is read in your browser and never uploaded.

  1. Close the game, then press Win + R and paste:
    %LOCALAPPDATA%\RSDragonwilds\Saved\SaveCharacters
  2. Pick the file named after your character, ending in .json (not .backup).

Quickest way: keep the SaveCharacters folder open in Explorer and drag your character file anywhere onto this page.

Covers quests, recipes, Sanguine Urns and skill levels. Mounts aren't readable from the save, so tick those yourself. Imports only add ticks; they never remove any.

Sanguine Urns absorbed
Max health bonus +0Each urn permanently adds 10 max health to your character, across every world.

Finding them

  • Urns show on your in-game map (a red potion in a spiked frame) once you've discovered that area.
  • Your compass picks one up from about 50–60 metres away.
  • Some are in caves or underground. None are inside the Dragonkin vaults.

Lore scraps read
Grouped by region, then by the story each scrap belongs to. Where it’s known, the area under each title tells you where it lies; Wiki has the exact spot.

Finding them

  • Lore scraps are notes, diaries and books lying on tables, shelves and the ground. Pick one up to add it to your journal.
  • Lore is saved on your character, not the world, so scraps you read in a friend’s world count for you too.
  • Statue sagas and songs from Umbral Sands quests aren't lore scraps, so they aren't listed here.

    Why 94?
